# BUG-002 — Pre-order product added to cart without stock warning on product page

**ID:** BUG-002  
**Jira:** OCT-36  
**Date:** 26/04/2026  
**Reported by:** Miguel Guedes  
**Status:** Open  

## Summary
Pre-order product added to cart without stock warning on product page.

## Environment
- Browser: Chrome (latest)
- OS: Windows 11
- URL: https://demo.opencart.com

## Severity / Priority
- Severity: Minor
- Priority: High

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Navigate to Samsung Galaxy Tab 10.1 product page
2. Observe availability shows "Pre-Order" with no warning
3. Click Add to Cart
4. Navigate to cart page
5. Product now shows *** and stock warning message

## Expected Result
Product page should clearly indicate stock limitations
before the user adds it to cart.

## Actual Result
Stock warning (***) only appears in cart after 
product is added, misleading the user.
